Workflow 1: AI Email Monitoring & Drafted Responses
Benefit
Reclaim 1–2 hours daily by eliminating inbox overload and decision fatigue.
Features
Real-Time Monitoring:
Connects to Gmail or Outlook to read incoming mail.
AI Classification:
Filters emails by priority and determines if a response is required.
Smart Drafting:
Generates professional replies in the client's specific brand voice.
Human Control:
Saves drafts directly in the inbox for final review (nothing sends without approval).
Priority Tagging:
Alerts users to urgent messages so nothing gets lost.
Sales Pitch Points
"Imagine having a personal executive assistant who reads every email and writes 90% of the response for you—for a fraction of the cost."
"Stop starting from a blank page. Our AI drafts the reply so you just have to click 'Send'."
"Eliminate 'Inbox Anxiety.' You'll never have to wonder if you missed an urgent client request again."
"This system saves you up to 10 hours a week—that is an entire workday given back to you every single week."
"It doesn't rob you of control; it gives you options. You always have the final say, but the heavy lifting is done for you."
